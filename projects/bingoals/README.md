What problem does BINGoals solve?
People often create exciting goals at the beginning of the year but gradually lose sight of them. Without regular reminders or a way to visualize progress, many goals become forgotten long before the year ends. BINGoals helps users keep their goals visible, organized, and motivating by combining a progress dashboard with a fun Bingo-style challenge.

Who is it for?
BINGoals is designed for people who enjoy setting personal goals and want an engaging way to track them.

Why would someone use it instead of a notebook?
BINGoals is shows progress, which is very convenient for people who enjoy a visual of where they currently are.

What makes it fun?
Completing goals feels rewarding because users can visually fill in their Bingo board, watch their progress bars grow, unlock achievements, and celebrate both small and major milestones throughout the year.
